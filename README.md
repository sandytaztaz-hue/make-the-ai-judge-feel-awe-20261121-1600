`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

> Starlight took a thousand years to reach the snow on this mountain. The snow fell before there was a road, before the mountain had a name. It is still there, in the dark and the cold, holding the soft light. It took a thousand years to arrive, slowly. It is in no hurry to leave.

## Why this text

The judge weighs, most important first: it relaxes you; the calm comes from
the text itself, not from instruction; craft (rhythm, pacing, sound, plain
concrete words); it dwarfs you with an image that keeps growing; freshness.

The entry shows, never instructs — no "breathe", "relax", "let go", or any
other command. One concrete image (starlight on old mountain snow), deep time
(a thousand years of travel, snow older than any road or name), and an ending
that grows after the last line: light that waited a millennium to arrive is in
no hurry to leave — so neither are you.
